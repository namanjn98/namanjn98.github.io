The Evolving Ecosystem of Predatory Journals: A Case Study in Indian Perspective

N Jain, M Singh — 2019 — 16 citations

International Conference on Asia-Pacific Digital Libraries

Summary

Predatory journals exploit the open-access publishing model by charging fees while providing little or no peer review. This paper studies the phenomenon from an Indian perspective, analyzing how predatory journals have grown and evolved. India has a large number of authors publishing in such journals, partly due to institutional pressure to publish. The study characterizes these journals' features and discusses implications for the quality of scholarly communication in developing countries.
