PlantDoc: A Dataset for Visual Plant Disease Detection

D Singh, N Jain, P Jain, P Kayal, S Kumawat, N Batra — 2020 — 963 citations

Proceedings of the 7th ACM IKDD CoDS and 25th COMAD

Summary

PlantDoc is a dataset for visual plant disease detection containing 2,569 data points across 13 plant species and up to 17 classes of diseases. Plant diseases account for an average of 30% loss in production in India. Most available datasets for plant disease detection were generated under controlled laboratory conditions and thus lack variability. PlantDoc was created by collecting images from the web and annotating them, making it more representative of real-world conditions. The paper benchmarks several deep learning models on this dataset.
