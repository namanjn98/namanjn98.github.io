TiZen: Neural Title Generation for Scientific Papers

H Jain, A Jain, C Maji, A Tiwari, N Jain — 2024 — 1 citation

Summary

TiZen is a neural system for automatically generating titles for scientific papers given their abstracts or content. Writing a good paper title is an art — it needs to be concise, informative, and engaging. TiZen uses sequence-to-sequence neural models trained on large corpora of papers to produce title suggestions. The system could assist researchers in drafting titles and potentially improve paper discoverability.
