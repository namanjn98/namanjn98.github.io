PredCheck: Detecting Predatory Behaviour in Scholarly World

MS Bedmutha, K Modi, K Patel, N Jain, M Singh — 2020 — 8 citations

Proceedings of the ACM/IEEE Joint Conference on Digital Libraries (JCDL), 2020

Summary

PredCheck is a system for automatically detecting predatory journals and conferences in the scholarly world. Predatory publishers accept papers with little to no peer review in exchange for publication fees, undermining academic integrity. PredCheck uses a combination of textual features, publisher metadata, and behavioral signals to classify whether a journal or conference is predatory. The system aims to help researchers identify trustworthy venues before submitting their work.
