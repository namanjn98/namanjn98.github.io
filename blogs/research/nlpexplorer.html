NLPExplorer: Exploring the Universe of NLP Papers

M Parmar, N Jain, P Jain, P Jayakrishna Sahit, S Pachpande, S Singh et al. — 2020 — 23 citations

European Conference on Information Retrieval (ECIR), 476–480

Summary

NLPExplorer is a search and exploration portal for NLP research papers. The system allows researchers to navigate the large and growing body of NLP literature through topic-based browsing, trend analysis, and keyword search. It aggregates papers from major venues and provides faceted exploration to help users discover relevant work. The tool addresses the challenge of information overload in a rapidly expanding research field.
