Bollyrics: Automatic Lyrics Generator for Romanised Hindi

N Jain, A Chauhan, A Chewale, O Mithbavkar, U Shah, M Singh — 2020

arXiv preprint arXiv:2007.12916

Summary

Bollyrics is a system for generating Bollywood song lyrics in Romanised Hindi (Hindi written using the Latin alphabet). Hindi song lyrics are rich in cultural references, poetic devices, and code-switching between Hindi and English. The work addresses the challenge of generating fluent, culturally appropriate lyrics using language models trained on a corpus of Bollywood songs. It explores multilingual and code-mixed text generation, a challenging problem for NLP in Indian languages.
