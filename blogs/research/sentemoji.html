SentEmoji: A Dataset to Generate Empathising Conversations

A Ravi, AKS Yadav, J Chauhan, J Dholakia, N Jain — 2020 — 2 citations

Proceedings of the 7th ACM IKDD CoDS and 25th COMAD

Summary

SentEmoji is a dataset designed for training models to generate empathetic conversational responses. Empathy is a key quality in dialogue systems, especially for mental health and support applications. The dataset pairs conversational utterances with emoji-enriched empathetic responses, using emojis as a proxy for emotional tone. The work explores how emojis can signal sentiment and guide response generation in affective dialogue systems.
