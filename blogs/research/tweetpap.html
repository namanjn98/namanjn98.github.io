TweetPap: A Dataset to Study the Social Media Discourse of Scientific Papers

N Jain, M Singh — 2021 — 4 citations

Proceedings of the ACM/IEEE Joint Conference on Digital Libraries (JCDL), 2021

Summary

TweetPap is a dataset capturing how scientific papers are discussed on Twitter. Social media has become an important channel for scientific communication, but the dynamics of how research spreads and is discussed online are not well understood. The dataset links tweets to their referenced papers, enabling studies on public engagement with science, misinformation, and how different research topics gain traction on social media. It supports tasks like altmetrics analysis and scientific discourse modeling.
