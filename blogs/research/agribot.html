Agribot: Agriculture-Specific Question Answer System

N Jain, P Jain, P Kayal, J Sahit, S Pachpande, J Choudhari, M Singh — 2025 — 135 citations

arXiv preprint arXiv:2509.21535

Summary

Agribot is a question answering system tailored for the agriculture domain. Farmers and agricultural workers often need quick, reliable answers to questions about crop diseases, fertilizers, weather patterns, and farming practices. Agribot leverages NLP techniques to answer such queries, drawing from domain-specific knowledge. The system demonstrates how language models can be adapted for specialized domains to serve communities with practical information needs.
